PIPELINE CHECK - ecagent (CS 498)
Run on 2026-11-11

This checks that the whole eval pipeline works before the real eval. It is NOT
the eval: one trial per agent on every task/profile pair, on draft fixtures,
with no manual grading.

SETUP
- Tasks: all 10 benchmark tasks, on the profiles each task is meant for
  (36 task/profile pairs).
- Runs: one trial per agent and pair, 108 trials in total.
  Total cost: $5.92.
- Data: one small synthetic database per task (week of Nov 16-22, 2026).
  "Today" is fixed to Nov 16. Web search off.
- Agents: full_agent, no_compact, single_pass (claude-sonnet-4-6).
- Grading: automatic checks against each task's answer key. Every pair has a
  correct answer, so a trial that picks nothing can't pass.

RESULTS
- full_agent: passed 19/36. Failed 3, picked nothing in 14.
- no_compact: passed 17/36. Failed 5, picked nothing in 14.
- single_pass: passed 18/36. Failed 15, picked nothing in 3.
- When they picked something: full_agent 19/22, no_compact 17/22,
  single_pass 18/33.
- No errors. Every pick was a real activity from the database.
- full_agent's calendar and registration steps worked in 90% of the trials
  where they were scored.

FINDINGS
1. full_agent and no_compact don't search when the request doesn't name an
   interest (e.g. "Can you find me an activity?"). They reply right away and
   tell the student their activity list is empty, which is false. This is all
   14 of their empty trials.
2. When they do search, both are accurate.
3. full_agent sometimes took back a correct pick after the student said yes,
   saying it had made the details up, and added nothing to the calendar.
4. single_pass always answers, but is wrong more often: outside the schedule,
   the wrong kind of activity, or off-interest.
5. full_agent and no_compact registered students for events they only asked to
   add to their calendar (26 trials).
6. Three bugs in the test setup were found and fixed along the way.
